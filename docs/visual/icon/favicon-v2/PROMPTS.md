# Built-in imagegen prompts

Two independent calls, transparent_background=true, no reference images.

## A — standalone

Use case: logo-brand. Create one original Moonveil tiny browser favicon master, square raster, genuinely transparent background, no text or mockups. Warm camping identity: bold crescent moon around a single campfire flame. Designed for 16 px first: fill almost the whole square, very chunky simple silhouette, only 5% outer padding. Broad crescent on left, compact flame nested on right; keep clear negative space between them. No stars, no texture, no gradients, no grain, no wood detail, no shadows, no extra ornaments, no third-party marks. Broad dark forest-teal contour around the ivory moon and warm amber flame so it works on light AND dark browser chrome. Flat clean confident curves. No tiny lines. Alternative A: standalone transparent silhouette, ivory crescent and one solid burnt-orange/gold flame, no logs at all; flame's broad fork and seated rounded base suggest a campfire. Absolutely no background plate or surrounding square. Shape mass occupies 90% of canvas.

## B — rounded plate

Use case: logo-brand. Single original Moonveil favicon master for 16/32/48 pixels, square raster with transparent outside. Two simple camping shapes: thick ivory crescent on left embracing one broad amber-orange campfire flame on right. Very compact bold flat design with a subtle dark forest-teal rounded-square plate with generous corner radius; plate fills 96% canvas, moon and flame fill 85% canvas, minimal internal padding. One broad inner golden flame only. No logs, grain, gradients, glow, stars, sparks, thin strokes, lettering, mockups, third-party logos. Polished original flat camping emblem. Keep distinct moon and flame negative space, substantial visual mass, designed to read at 16 pixels. True transparency outside the rounded plate, not white corners. A restrained alternative to a standalone silhouette, same warm ivory/amber and forest palette.
